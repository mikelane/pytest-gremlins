## Segments

<!-- SEGMENT: intro -->
pytest-gremlins keeps a history of every run — so you can see at a glance whether your mutation score is climbing, holding, or quietly slipping.

<!-- SEGMENT: first_run -->
First run. One point on the chart, 63 percent. That's your baseline.

<!-- SEGMENT: second_run -->
Second run. Score is still 63 — the chart now has two points. Consistent results, nothing surprising.

<!-- SEGMENT: third_run -->
Third run, same score. Three runs flat. That kind of stability means your tests are holding the line.

<!-- SEGMENT: trend_chart -->
Down in the history section you get the full picture — three data points, nearly a straight line. This is what a healthy test suite looks like over time.

<!-- SEGMENT: regression -->
Fourth run — but a test got removed. Score drops. The chart catches it immediately: one dip, right there, exactly when it happened.

<!-- SEGMENT: outro -->
One glance at the trend and you know if things are getting better or drifting. pytest-gremlins surfaces that automatically, every single run.
