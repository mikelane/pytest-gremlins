## Segments

<!-- SEGMENT: intro -->
When you open a pytest-gremlins report, it comes up dark by default. No settings, no config — just dark, with the gremlin-green scores standing out right away.

<!-- SEGMENT: dark_mode -->
Here it is at full width. The mutation scores are front and center, styled so you can read the results fast, even after a long test run.

<!-- SEGMENT: toggle -->
Up in the corner there's a theme toggle. One click and you're in light mode.

<!-- SEGMENT: persist -->
Now reload the page. It comes back in light mode — no flash, no reset. The preference is saved in local storage.

<!-- SEGMENT: mobile -->
At 375 pixels — phone width — everything stacks cleanly. You can scroll vertically. No horizontal overflow.

<!-- SEGMENT: outro -->
Dark by default, one-click toggle, and it remembers your choice. It also just works on a phone. Small things, but they're the difference between a report you actually check and one you ignore.
